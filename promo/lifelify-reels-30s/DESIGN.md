# lifelify Reels Promo Design

## Style Prompt

Build a vertical social promo that feels like the actual lifelify iOS app: bright iOS surfaces, soft material cards, bold black Japanese headings, precise daily data, and friendly habit/health color. The mood is clear, capable, and gently motivating. Motion should feel like moving through a polished iPhone interface rather than a loud product launch.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#F7F9FC` soft iOS off-white.
- Ink: `#050507` strong app typography and icon color.
- Muted text: `#8A8F98` secondary labels.
- Card: `#FFFFFF` with soft shadow, matching SwiftUI thin material.
- Accent blue: `#0A84FF` for primary actions and tab emphasis.
- Habit green: `#22C55E` for progress and heatmaps.
- Calendar orange: `#F97316` for work/category markers.
- Health purple: `#7C3AED` for sleep and wellness analysis.

## Typography

- Japanese UI/display: `Hiragino Sans`, heavy weights for headings, regular for body.
- Data/numbers: `SF Mono`, `Menlo`, monospace with tabular numerals.
- Keep text large for mobile video: 72px+ headlines, 30px+ support copy, 24px+ labels.

## Motion

- Primary transition: horizontal or vertical push slide, 0.45-0.55s.
- Wind-down transition: blur crossfade into the final CTA.
- Entrances: every scene animates in with varied y, x, scale, and opacity, offset 0.18s+.
- Ambient: subtle phone float, progress-cell pulses, and slow panel drift. No infinite repeats.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use generic dark neon/purple-blue gradients.
- Do not replace the real app screenshots with abstract-only UI.
- Do not overcrowd small text; favor short Japanese captions.
- Do not use jump cuts between scenes.
- Do not imply user-created diary content is translated or overwritten.
